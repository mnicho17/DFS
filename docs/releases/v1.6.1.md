# DFS Optimizer v1.6.1

Version 1.6.1 connects NFL SIM predictions to actual contest results and adds visual references to the user guide.

## Highlights

- NFL SIM exports retain their original Edge, top-one-percent, top-five-percent, cash, return, leverage, duplication, scenario-count, and representative-field estimates.
- **Results & Learning** compares those saved predictions with exact matched DraftKings results.
- The learning report checks predicted versus actual top-one-percent, top-five-percent, and cash rates.
- It reports whether SIM Edge tracks finish percentile and whether the tournament return index tracks net results.
- SIM validation remains clearly labeled directional until at least 50 matched entries are available.
- The downloadable PDF guide now includes real app-reference images for the main NFL workflow, Portfolio Rules, Classic SIM results, and Results & Learning.

## Download

Download `DFS-Optimizer.exe` and, if desired, verify it with `DFS-Optimizer.exe.sha256`. The release also includes the illustrated `DFS-Optimizer-User-Guide.pdf`.

## Important notes

- Only exact lineups previously exported from the app can be matched to imported DraftKings results.
- The Windows executable is not code-signed, so Windows may show an unknown-publisher warning.
- SIM and learning outputs are decision aids, not guarantees or automatic strategy recommendations.
- Run **Game-Day Check** close to lock and review every exported entry before submitting it.
