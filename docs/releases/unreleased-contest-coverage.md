# Contest objectives and QB fade coverage — development preview

Single entry no longer implies a contest objective. Choose Tournament, Double-Up
or Multiplier explicitly, independently of requested lineup count and entry-limit
preset. Exact-profile Double-Up selection prioritizes paid-finish rate; Multiplier
and Tournament profiles prioritize expected profit using the entered payouts and
tie splits. NFL SIM is required, with Deep for Showdown. Missing cash economics
or scoring stops execution. Default Tournament builds remain unchanged.

QB Coverage adds explicit starting-QB fade minimums, both/either/neither counts,
Captain exposure, WR/TE dependencies and a cancellable paired conditional point
review. Targets stay hard; no automatic cap recovery weakens existing rules.
The point review discloses uniform pre-exit production and remaining WR/TE
efficiency. It supplies neither injury probabilities nor cash coverage. Backup
allocation, RB receiving and replacement opportunity models remain future work.

This preview has not been merged or released. Verification is recorded in the
review PR. Historical observations, ordinary forecasts and disabled-build
behavior remain protected by isolated regression and Tournament fixtures.
