# DFS Optimizer v1.13.0

## Deep Build for NFL Classic

- Added **Build depth: Deep (up to 5 min)** alongside the unchanged Fast default.
- Deep explores up to 6,000 candidates using four optimizer seeds, field-shaped builds, and correlated Ceiling, Balanced, Leverage, and Low-Dup scenarios.
- A coarse SIM screens the expanded bank, preserves candidate-source and scenario-archetype diversity, and passes a smaller shortlist to independent validation.
- Final validation uses a separate random stream, at least 2,500 scenarios, and a larger representative field.

## Better five-minute use

- Deep reserves time for final selection instead of allowing candidate generation to consume the whole budget.
- If the time budget or Cancel is reached, the strongest completed stage remains available for portfolio selection.
- A local swap pass can improve the finished portfolio while rechecking player groups, minimum uniqueness, player limits, and team/game maximums.
- Fast remains the default and keeps its existing runtime and candidate budget.

## Progress and diagnostics

- Deep progress clearly identifies Explore, Screen, Validate, and Select/Refine.
- Build reports record the expanded bank size, shortlist size, screening scenarios, independent validation scenarios, top-candidate agreement between the two SIM passes, refinement swaps, and time-budget status.
- Added regression coverage for shortlist diversity, time-bounded refinement, Deep controls, and aggregate reporting.

Deep Build provides a wider and more independently tested search, but its outputs still depend on projection, ownership, player-status, and field-model assumptions. Always complete a final news and entry review before lock.
