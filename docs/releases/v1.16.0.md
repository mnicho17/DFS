# DFS Optimizer v1.16.0

## Final Lock Check

- NFL export now refreshes player status immediately before the file-save step.
- Late changes and currently unavailable players are mapped to the exact saved lineup numbers they affect.
- **Replace Affected Lineups** keeps unaffected saved rows fixed and generates only the required replacements.
- If the live source cannot be confirmed, the app labels the cached-data fallback clearly instead of implying the check succeeded.

## Platform-valid lineup safety

- Classic and Showdown generation now enforce athletes from at least two teams; Showdown must use exactly the two teams in one game.
- Entry Safety blocks the same athlete appearing twice, including position plus FLEX and Captain plus FLEX.
- The exact export is checked for roster positions, salary cap, positive salary data, current-slate membership, unique and slot-specific DraftKings IDs, duplicate entries, availability, and current portfolio rules.
- **Replace Blocked Lineups** repairs only the exact blocked rows while preserving the remainder of the saved portfolio.
- Questionable players, uncertain live data, and intentional salary leverage remain visible review items rather than silent changes.

## Documentation

- The guide adds a visual Final Lock Check reference and expands the export checklist.
- The five-minute workflow now covers late-news replacement and platform-valid roster checks.
